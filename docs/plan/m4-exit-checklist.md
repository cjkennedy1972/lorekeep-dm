## Recorded decisions

- **2026-10-26 — One DM narration per resolved turn.** A combat move alone does not narrate. Narration starts when the turn resolves (for example on `end-turn`). This is by design under ADR-033 §6.3 (D1), and it resolves the open question raised in the #337 evidence report.
