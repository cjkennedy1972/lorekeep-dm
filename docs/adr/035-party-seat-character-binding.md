# ADR-035: Party-seat character binding and late-join PC entry

Status: Proposed · Date: 2026-10-23 · Owner: Sage · Scope: M4 party seat identity, character ownership, and late-join PC entry

Append-only: this ADR adds a decision; it does not amend existing ADR bodies. It is authoritative for the party binding gaps identified by M4-4 PR #278. It complements ADR-029 through ADR-034.

## Context

Spec §3.2 US-P3 says each seat has exactly one active PC, and §3.2 US-P5 admits a player at a safe point. Spec §4.2 models `Seat {accountId,...,pcId?}`, but no implemented party binding exists: `sessions.character_id` and `sessions.character` are the solo-session binding. SeatJoined carries account identity, not a durable selected PC. Combat events and initiative refer to engine `entityId`/`activeEntityId`, not accounts or seats. Therefore round submission identity (ADR-029), initiative/turn ownership and reactions (ADR-033/034), rest voting (ADR-030), and defensive autopilot (ADR-031) cannot reliably resolve a party PC. Late-join approval alone does not make the entrant combat-ready.

## Decision

### 1. Ownership model and binding authority

Add `session_seat_characters` (name provisional; schema implementation may use `seat_characters`) as the durable binding and snapshot record: `session_id`, stable `seat_id`, `account_id`, `character_id`, `character_revision`, `character_snapshot` (validated canonical character state), `bound_at`, `unbound_at`, and `binding_status` (`active|released|redacted`). Keep party binding out of `sessions.character_id`; that field remains solo-only.

Enforce one active character per seat and one active seat per character within a session using partial unique constraints on `(session_id, seat_id)` and `(session_id, character_id)` where active. An account may own any number of characters and seats; it may not bind a character it does not own. Distinct seats may not share one character. This is a per-session exclusivity rule, not global character exclusivity.

The account that owns the seat selects/binds its own character at join or in lobby, before the first round opens. The host may admit/kick and may start only when every active player seat is bound and ready; host approval of the character choice is not required. For a late join, the requester supplies the character selection with the join request. The host approves admission after seeing only its public character card; the binding is created only when admission is approved. Unbound seats remain lobby/pending and cannot submit round actions, vote, or control combat.

On binding, validate ownership, current character legality, supported rules/catalog version, and absence of another live binding. Copy a canonical character sheet snapshot into this session binding. The immutable snapshot is specifically the rules input sheet used to initialize/derive the engine entity (identity/build fields such as abilities, level, class features, and starting equipment); it is not the live combat state. Later edits to the account character affect future bindings only. HP, conditions, position, initiative, spent actions/reactions, spell slots, hit dice, and other expendable resources are session-owned engine state and evolve through the normal events (including `HpChanged`, `ConditionApplied`, `ConditionRemoved`, `ActionSpent`, `BonusActionSpent`, `ReactionResolved`, `SlotSpent`, and `ConcentrationDropped` where applicable). Such events update the session projection, never rewrite the immutable sheet snapshot or reusable source character. Replay uses the snapshot plus subsequent session-state events, not current character rows. This follows ADR-003's event/snapshot replay model; ADR-016's in-flight snapshot rule applies to narration safety state, not character sheets.

### 2. Lifecycle, deletion, and combat departure

- **Leave/kick/revoke:** revoke seat control under ADR-031/029 and mark the binding `released` at that same serialized transition. The character becomes available for another seat/session immediately; the historical entity and events remain replayable. If the same account is later authorized into the same live session as a new seat, it may automatically re-bind the same character only after the Room re-checks that the account still owns it and it is not actively bound elsewhere in that session; otherwise it must explicitly select another eligible character. Ownership is checked again at the serialized rebind commit, not trusted from the earlier binding or join request. Re-entry never silently inherits a released binding. Rebind creates a new binding interval on that character's retained session entity/state; it does not transfer the retired seat's control. A different character requires a new explicit selection.
- **Departure during combat:** retain the PC entity, HP, position, initiative, and history. On confirmed away/revocation, immediately decline its pending reactions as ADR-031 requires. On its turn, use ADR-031's Dodge/hold defensive autopilot (and its narrowed M4 behavior), then advance initiative normally. Do not despawn or transfer the PC to another seat. A kicked/revoked seat cannot regain control without a new host-approved admission; any later re-entry follows the explicit re-bind rule above, never an implicit transfer.
- **Character deletion:** block deletion while any active binding exists. Deletion after release deletes the reusable character source; it does not mutate a running session snapshot. If deletion is legally required during a live session, redact source ownership and apply account-deletion scrub rules; preserve only the minimum session PC snapshot needed to keep game state coherent, with the anonymization behavior below.
- **Account deletion:** follow spec US-A4 AC3 and ADR-017/028 retention/deletion. Gameplay events carry stable session-local `seatId`/engine `entityId` references and gameplay facts only—never account IDs, reusable `characterId`, character source fields, private revision, or snapshot hash. Binding persistence may hold the account/character foreign keys while active, but the account-deletion job atomically revokes seats, nulls those links, and replaces any sheet-bearing binding row with a redacted/tombstone state that retains only the anonymous engine initialization mechanics required to replay the session (not source identity/build metadata that is not needed for mechanics). If legacy/derived event payloads contain identifying links, the same authorized redaction job uses the sanctioned append-only exception (`redact_event`) to replace only sensitive fields; retain the event type, sequence, timestamp, seat/entity identifiers, and non-identifying mechanics needed for replay. This uses the named account-deletion redaction job authorized by ADR-003 and run by the ADR-017 retention-sweeper/account-deletion pipeline; shared-session gameplay remains live while active, is archived after 14 idle days, and is deleted 90 days after archive unless claimed. Binding and gameplay events feed that job through stable `seatId`/`entityId` references and explicit sensitive-field classification; the job scrubs only account/character identifiers and sheet/private fields, preserving event type, sequence, timestamp, and mechanics needed for replay. This is the narrow account-deletion redaction exception in ADR-003/017, consistent with the payload-free tombstone precedent in migration `0036`; it is not ordinary event mutation. Replay must remain authoritative from the retained gameplay projection and tombstones must be defined as no-op metadata for that event's sensitive projection, never as a missing sequence. The session may continue with an anonymous DM-controlled PC/NPC, consistent with spec; do not retain an account-to-character link. This ADR does not redefine the retention clocks.
- **Character swap:** no swap while a session is active. Between sessions, reuse any owned character that has no active binding. A replacement PC in a live session requires a separate explicit product/engine flow and must not overwrite the old event history.

### 3. Late join and legal spawn

Host approval remains required by spec §6.5 and ADR-031. Admission proceeds: request with selected character → validate ownership/revision and present public card → host approval or decline → establish the authorized seat and presence per ADR-031 Seat claim and entry item 2 (including snapshot acknowledgment and `returning` state) → deliver table settings and mature opt-out/onboarding → create binding → scene boundary/next between-round gate → legal placement → playable-membership/join commit. Seat authorization and presence therefore precede binding; binding and legal placement precede playable membership, action eligibility, and exposure of a playable PC. An approved but incomplete join remains pending and has no gameplay control. Deliver the current table safety/content settings and a ≤150-word recap (spec US-S3) before the join commit; the joiner may opt out before any subsequent narration, per ADR-016.

Spawn only at a scene boundary or between combat rounds, never during an active turn/reaction window. The rules engine computes an available legal spawn cell from the scene's explicit player-entry spawn zone; if none is authored, use the nearest unoccupied legal cell to the party's designated entry anchor, tie-broken by row then column. Validate occupancy, terrain, map bounds, size, and reachability using ADR-018 engine rules. If no legal cell exists, keep the approved join pending until the next safe boundary or ask the host to select an engine-validated entry zone; never invent coordinates on the client/LLM. If the host declines, the request expires/times out, the player withdraws, or the join is otherwise canceled before commit, serialize cancellation in the Room, emit `SeatCharacterReleased` when a binding had been made, release its uniqueness claim, revoke/close the pending seat as appropriate under ADR-031, and discard the pending spawn reservation. If it remains pending awaiting a future legal spawn, retain the binding only while the approved seat/request remains live; on timeout or revocation release it as above. Late PC joins initiative at the next round start, not retroactively in the current round; exploration inclusion begins at the join commit.

### 4. Events, replay, commands, and authorization

Append events `SeatCharacterBound{seatId}` (identifier-only; no account/character ID, revision, hash, or sheet data), `SeatCharacterReleased{seatId, reason}`, and `LateJoinSpawned{seatId, entityId, sceneId, pos, initiativeEffectiveRound?}`. Persist the private binding row and immutable sheet snapshot transactionally with the identifier-only binding event. For a new approved seat, follow ADR-031's order: seat authorization/`SeatJoined` and snapshot acknowledgment establish `returning` presence first; then persist binding; only after binding and legal placement may the seat become playable/active party membership, and `RoundOpened` follows readiness. For initial lobby seats the same principle applies: presence/seat claim, then bind, then readiness/start (no gameplay command eligibility before binding). For late join, request-time character selection and validation precede host approval; approval creates the authorized seat and presence; binding is persisted before the boundary `EntityPlaced`/`LateJoinSpawned`, then playable join commit; the next `RoundOpened` follows commit. Replay consumes the immutable binding snapshot plus gameplay events and identifier-only tombstones; it never rereads mutable character rows. `SeatCharacterBound` is not a broadcast payload: the public stream gets only a projected public character card/readiness update, while the owning seat receives revision/snapshot metadata over a seat-private authorized channel. The private revision/hash are never sent to other seats or host.

WebSocket commands: `BindCharacter{characterId, expectedRevision, actionId}`, `RequestLateJoin{characterId, expectedRevision, actionId}`, and `ApproveLateJoin/DeclineLateJoin`. Initial binding and late-join request selection run through the Room serial mailbox and use idempotency keys; late-join selection is submitted with the request, not after approval. Every mutation checks current authenticated socket ticket, current non-revoked seat, account status, seat-account ownership, character ownership, session status, and binding uniqueness at commit time (ADR-029 §7 style; no trust in client-supplied account/seat/entity IDs). Host-only approval; seat owner alone chooses its character. Use existing join/control gate and per-seat mutation budget (ADR-029 §8); character binding consumes that budget. Because an unaffiliated requester has no seat budget, late-join requests also use a token bucket keyed by account (burst 3, then 1 request per 2 minutes), source IP (10 per 10 minutes), and invite code when present (5 per 10 minutes); each session allows at most 3 pending requests total and 1 pending request per account. Exceeding any limit or pending cap returns a neutral `429 request_limited` response with `Retry-After` when known; it creates no seat, presence, binding, or persisted text. Reject stale revisions, cross-account selection, duplicate live bindings, and invalid state with neutral typed errors; do not persist rejected payloads. Repeated equivalent `actionId` returns its prior result.

### 5. Visibility and privacy

All seated clients may see bound character display name, class, ancestry/species, level, combat entity ID, position, initiative, and public HP/conditions as game state requires. Only the owning account may see/edit its reusable source character. Other seats do not receive source character revision, backstory/private notes, account identifiers, or raw binding ownership metadata. Host approval sees only the proposed public character card needed for admission (display name, class, and level), not private account profile fields or a durable binding. Split transport explicitly: the all-seat public `CharacterCard`/readiness projection contains only the approved public fields; the owning seat's private binding acknowledgment may include `characterRevision` and `snapshotHash`; neither the persisted identifier-only event nor any all-seat broadcast includes those private values. Account/character foreign keys and snapshot remain server-only. Account deletion scrubs identifiers while preserving only approved gameplay projection.

### 6. Migration and compatibility

No backfill from `sessions.character_id` to party seats: it is solo-only and ownership cannot safely be inferred for historical party events. Existing solo sessions remain unchanged and continue using `sessions.character_id`/`character`. Existing party seats with no binding are explicitly `unbound`; they cannot enter a new active round/combat until their owner binds a character. For already-active legacy parties, deploy a compatibility lobby gate: pause round opening, prompt each seat to bind once, then resume; do not guess or assign the solo owner character. Old SeatJoined events remain valid with no binding, and replay projects the seat as unbound. Additive nullable schema/migration first; new Room code tolerates missing binding records during rollout, while new gameplay mutations require binding. Rollback is non-destructive: never run a down migration that drops binding rows or events. With the feature flag off, reject new party starts that need binding and new late joins/binds with a neutral unavailable response; preserve all existing bindings, snapshots, and events. Active Rooms already using the feature must drain under the compatible code; if that code is unavailable, pause those Rooms safely rather than fall back to solo `sessions.character_id`, discard bindings, or reinterpret party entities. Re-enable only after the compatible code and feature flag are restored.

### 7. Alternatives considered

1. Put `character_id` directly on `SeatJoined` or `sessions`: rejected; event payload is not a mutable ownership store, and sessions-level ownership is wrong for multiple seats.
2. Reuse `sessions.character_id` for the host/player party: rejected; it is solo-only and cannot represent 2–6 distinct PCs.
3. Store only a character ID and load live account character data during play: rejected; later edits/deletion would rewrite running-session behavior and break replay.
4. Let host pick any seat's character or auto-assign a pregenerated character: rejected; removes account ownership/control and lacks product authorization.
5. Allow shared characters or mid-session swaps: rejected by default because resource/turn ownership becomes ambiguous and replay history forks.

## Ordered implementation breakdown

1. **Forge — schema and persistence.** Add additive migration, constraints, repository operations, character ownership/revision validation, immutable session snapshot, deletion/revocation scrubbing. Acceptance: concurrency attempts cannot create duplicate live seat/character bindings; solo paths and old unbound SeatJoined replay unchanged; account deletion leaves no account-character link and does not corrupt gameplay replay.
2. **Forge — Room identity projection and events.** Project seat→entity→snapshot into Room state; emit/replay binding/release events; gate every gameplay command against bound seat and current authorization. Acceptance: each accepted round action/rest ballot/reaction/control resolves the acting seat's one entity; cross-seat/client-forged IDs rejected; replay after restart yields identical ownership.
3. **Prism — lobby binding/readiness.** Owner-selectable character picker, readiness, public card, stale revision/ownership errors, solo compatibility. Acceptance: 2–6 seats bind distinct owned PCs; start blocked for unbound seats; another account cannot inspect/select source data.
4. **Forge + Prism — late join safe-entry flow.** Integrate request/host approval from #278 with settings/onboarding/recap, character binding, scene-boundary spawn selection, and next-round initiative. Acceptance: no current-round turn insertion; map occupancy/legal-cell invariant holds; settings/opt-out and recap precede first post-entry narration; no spawn yields pending, not unsafe placement.
5. **Proof — acceptance/regression suite.** Cover 2–6 seat binding, same-account multiple characters across sessions, duplicate binding race, edits after snapshot, leave/kick/rejoin, away turn/autopilot/reaction decline, character/account deletion, late join at exploration and combat boundaries, no legal spawn, event replay, legacy solo/unbound seats, and all authorization/rate limits. Acceptance: deterministic event traces match across replay and deletion tests assert scrubbed identifiers; a concurrency test races release/rebind against a late-join selection for the same character and proves the serialized winner is unique, ownership-checked, and replay-consistent.
6. **Bastion — adversarial/privacy review.** Verify tenant isolation, forged identifiers, stale tickets/revoked seats, private character fields, retention/deletion card compatibility, and no private event data in broadcasts. Acceptance: review findings resolved or explicitly tracked before M4 gate.

## M4 dependency impact and open product questions

This decision is a prerequisite for M4 items **2** (action identity and eligible PCs), **4** (late join, leave/rejoin, PC autopilot), **6** (combat turn/reaction ownership), **7** (map entities/monster-vs-PC distinction), and **8** (party/map UI). Thus items 2, 4, 6, 7, and 8 are blocked on the binding contract/integration for their complete acceptance criteria. Ownership remains as listed in `docs/plan/m4-breakdown.md`: 2 Forge + Proof; 4 Forge + Prism + Proof; 6 Forge + Proof; 7 Forge + Proof; 8 Prism. Item 5 (Forge + Proof + Prism) may implement vote mechanics independently against seat IDs but is blocked from acceptance until binding authorization is integrated. Item 1 (Forge + Bastion, Prism UI) and item 3 (Prism + Forge) may proceed independently; item 3's readiness/start criteria depend on binding. Item 0 contract owner is Sage.

**Needs owner confirmation (recommended defaults apply meanwhile):**

- Can two seats play the same pregenerated character? **Default: no; distinct character records and live binding per session.**
- Can a seat change characters between sessions? **Default: yes, select any owned character not actively bound elsewhere in that session.**
- Can a player replace a character mid-session? **Default: no; retire/leave then use a separately designed replacement flow.**
- Is host approval of the character selection required? **Default: no; host approves admission only, owner controls its PC.**

## Consequences

Party character ownership becomes explicit, auditable, and replay-stable; solo sessions retain their existing contract. The binding snapshot adds storage and deletion-scrub obligations. M4 cannot claim complete party combat or late-join PC entry until the ordered work above is integrated.
